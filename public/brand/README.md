# Critter Connect's brand files

These are Critter Connect's own logo and icons, copied unchanged from the
game's repository, [kc0588615/phaser-june](https://github.com/kc0588615/phaser-june)
at commit `75799a8`:

- `critterconnect-logo-light.svg`: the lockup for light backgrounds
- `critterconnect-logo.svg`: the lockup for dark backgrounds
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`: the favicon
- `app-icon-512.png`: the app icon

They're the game's brand, not part of critwire's code, and the repository's
MIT licence doesn't cover them. A copy of critwire that isn't Critter
Connect's site replaces them, and `SITE` in `src/lib/site.ts`, with its own.
