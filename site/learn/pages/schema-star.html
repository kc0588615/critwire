Star schema: what Power BI reads

A star schema built from the lab data for reporting: CSV files under /learn/data/star/, which Power BI Desktop loads by URL. The database architecture track builds the same star in SQL.

A star puts the numbers to add up in fact tables, one row per event at a fixed grain, and the words to filter and group by in dimension tables around them. fact_records has one row per sample record. fact_species_country has no measure at all: a row says that a species has open records in a country, and counting rows is the analysis (a factless fact).

Both share dim_species and dim_country. A dimension two facts share is conformed: one slicer on species or country filters both. The habitat fact, fact_country_habitat, shares only dim_country; it has no species.

Every foreign key in a fact is filled. A record with no year points at dim_year's -1 row, and one in no country at dim_country's ZZ row: an unknown member. So every line is "exactly one" on the dimension's side, and no record falls out of a report because a key was empty.
